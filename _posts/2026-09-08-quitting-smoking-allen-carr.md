This is my first post, and it is not about software. ✍️

For years I thought cigarettes helped with stress, focus, and breaks. Allen Carr's *Easy Way to Stop Smoking* flipped that story: nicotine creates a small itch, then sells you the relief of scratching it. The "pleasure" is mostly ending the discomfort the last cigarette caused. 💡

Carr calls that physical itch the **little monster**. It dies in a few days. The **big monster** is the belief that smoking does something useful. Willpower fights cravings while that belief stays intact. Easy Way attacks the belief first. Once the cigarette stops looking like a friend, you are not giving anything up — you are getting out. 🚪

I was struggling to get my focus and happiness back without smoking. I am not a doctor, and one post is not a substitute for the book — but if the method clicks for you, read Carr's book all the way through, then stop. No mourning. No "just one." Celebrate immediately. 🎉
